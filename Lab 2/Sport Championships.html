About the Sports

Football

A team sport played between two teams of 11 players, where the main objective is to score by kicking or heading a ball into the opponent's goal without using hands or arms.

Badminton

A racket sport played by either two opposing players (singles) or two opposing pairs (doubles), who score points by striking a shuttlecock with their rackets so that it passes over the net and lands in the opponent's half of the court.

Volleyball

A team sport where two teams of six players are separated by a net, and each team tries to score points by grounding a ball on the opponent's court using their hands or arms within three allowed touches.

100m run

A premier track and field event where athletes sprint a distance of 100 meters in a straight line, testing pure human speed, explosive power, and acceleration to the absolute limit.

Esports

A form of competitive video gaming where professional players or teams compete against each other in organized tournaments, requiring high strategic thinking, lightning-fast reflexes, and teamwork.

Rugby

A high-contact team sport played with an oval-shaped ball, where the main objective is to score points by carrying, passing, and kicking the ball over the opponent's goal line to score a try, or by kicking it through the goalposts.
