Masjid Kristal

Constructed from steel, glass, and crystal, this stunning architectural landmark sits right on the Terengganu River inside the Islamic Heritage Park. It is one of the state's most iconic photo spots, especially when illuminated at night.

Rantau Abang

Historically famous as the primary nesting beach for giant leatherback turtles, this coastal center now operates an educational gallery and hatchery. Visitors can learn about marine turtle conservation, view live hatchlings in tanks, and walk along the long scenic shoreline.

Only RM 299

Bukit Kluang

Rising dramatically right next to the coast, this scenic coastal hill is famous for its ancient sea caves, wooden coastal boardwalk, and easy hiking trail. A short 30-minute climb rewards hikers with breathtaking panoramic views of the turquoise sea and nearby Perhentian Islands.

Only RM 250
